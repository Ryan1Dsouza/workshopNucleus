Use your phone as a controller

  1. Install the Android app

    Download the APK on your phone, open it and tap Install. If asked, allow your browser to install this app.

    Download Android APK

  2. Start Bluetooth

    Open ControllerKit on your phone, tap Start Bluetooth and allow Nearby devices when prompted.

  3. Pair on your laptop

    Open this site in Chrome or Edge. Under Player connections, click Connect phone, select your phone, then choose a game.
