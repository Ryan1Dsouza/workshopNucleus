# Interface fonts

ControllerKit bundles the unmodified Latin WOFF2 subsets of Nunito Sans and Source Sans 3. They load locally in the browser and are embedded in the offline Android/iPhone interface. No font service is contacted at runtime.

- Source Sans 3: Copyright 2010–2020 Adobe. See SourceSans3-OFL.txt.
